WDD 131 – Week 4 Project: Website Subject

1.	Subject
Youngchi Empire – Cybersecurity and Digital Protection.

2.	Content
I want to create a website about Youngchi Empire, a project that focuses on cybersecurity 
and protecting digital information. The website will explain what cybersecurity is, common 
online threats, and simple ways people can protect their devices and personal information. 
It will also introduce the Youngchi Empire project and some of its features.

3.	Reason
I chose this topic because I have always been interested in technology, computers, 
and cybersecurity. I want to learn more about how people can stay safe online while 
improving my skills in web development. I also think this website will give me an opportunity 
to share useful information that can help others protect themselves on the internet.
